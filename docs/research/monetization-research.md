# CampList monetization research (as of 2026-10-07)

Scope: camplist.guide — static, privacy-respecting camping checklist web app, <1,000 monthly visits at launch, US owner. Audience: campers, backpackers, overlanders, festival-goers. Every factual claim links its source; "(third-party, unverified)" marks claims not confirmed on an official page.

## Bottom line

- Display ads at this traffic level are worth roughly $5–15/month (1,000 pageviews × a $5–15 page RPM), and AdSense is unlikely to approve a single-page tool with little text. Premium networks are out of reach: Journey needs 1,000 Tier-1 sessions/30 days, Raptive 25k pageviews, Ezoic 250k monthly users for new sites, Carbon/EthicalAds are developer-only.
- Affiliate links fit a checklist better (Amazon 3% on Outdoors/Sports/Tools, Backcountry 4–12%, REI ~5%), but need a per-placement "paid link"-style disclosure and must satisfy Amazon's April 2026 "original content with commentary" rule.
- A $24/yr storage tier at 1 GB/user nets ~$1.89/user/month after Stripe and Cloudflare R2 (≈94% gross margin); a $1.99/mo plan loses ~19% to Stripe's fixed $0.30.
- Rewarded ads on the web exist only through Google Ad Manager (which requires an AdSense account). Never pay for reviews.

**Amazon rules that matter for a checklist app**
- Three qualifying sales within 180 days of applying or "your application can be withdrawn"; IDs are not reinstated ([Account dormancy](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G7MJTPEP9NC3YKMG)).
- Required statement: "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." ([Operating Agreement §5](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement)).
- Changes effective Apr 14, 2026: purchases must ship/stream and be paid within 180 days; purchases referred through paid or boosted ads are disqualified; "Original Content" must contain "commentary, analysis or transformation"; the same-ASIN restriction applies to Onsite commissions only, not offsite links ([What's Changed](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/compare)). A bare list of product links may not qualify as original content — add a sentence of real commentary per linked item.

**Sponsored templates — FTC disclosure**
- The 2023 Endorsement Guides add a definition of "clear and conspicuous" and warn that a platform's built-in disclosure tool may be inadequate ([FTC, June 29, 2023](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/06/federal-trade-commission-announces-updated-advertising-guides-combat-deceptive-reviews-endorsements)).
- FTC staff Q&A: "Paid link" next to an affiliate link is adequate; "affiliate link" alone and "commissionable link" are not; "Sponsored by XYZ" (with the brand name) is clearer than "Sponsored"; "A single disclosure on your home page won't be sufficient" ([FTC Q&A](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking)).
- Practical pattern: on a sponsored template card and inside the template, a top-of-content label "Sponsored by [Brand]" in the body font size; on each affiliate item, "Paid link" or "I earn a commission from this link"; a separate disclosure page is supplementary, not a substitute.

## 3. Consent and privacy

**EEA/UK/Switzerland visitors with AdSense**
- Google's EU User Consent Policy requires consent for cookies/local storage and for use of personal data for ad personalization, retained consent records, and "clear instructions for revocation of consent" ([policy](https://www.google.com/about/company/user-consent-policy/)).
- A Google-certified TCF CMP is required for personalized ads since Jan 16, 2024 (EEA/UK) and Jul 31, 2024 (CH); Google's own European regulations message in AdSense "Privacy & messaging" is certified; without a certified CMP, traffic "may be eligible for non-personalized ads or limited ads" only ([Google](https://support.google.com/admanager/answer/13554116)). Setup is in AdSense → Privacy & messaging → GDPR ([AdSense](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/14325056)).
- TCF v2.3 strings are mandatory from Mar 1, 2026; invalid strings default to Limited ads ([Google TCF page](https://support.google.com/admanager/answer/9805023)).

**US visitors**
- CCPA applies to for-profit businesses with >$25M revenue, or that buy/sell/share personal information of 100,000+ Californians, or derive ≥50% of revenue from selling/sharing personal information; covered businesses must post a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link and honor Global Privacy Control ([California AG](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa)). The 50%-of-revenue test could capture an ad-funded site; whether AdSense's data flows count as "sharing" at this scale is a legal question — flagged, not resolved here.
- Google's restricted data processing (RDP) stops profile-based ad personalization; partners "may choose to enable restricted data processing when they receive a GPC opt-out signal" ([Google RDP](https://business.safety.google/rdp/)). AdSense's US state regulations message supports IAB GPP strings for US National, CA, CO, CT, FL, VA and triggers RDP on opt-out ([AdSense](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/14126816)). Simplest low-tracking path: enable RDP/non-personalized ads for US users and pass GPC.

**Privacy-friendly analytics (no banner per vendors)**
- Plausible: from $9/month at 10k pageviews, 30-day trial, "doesn't process personal data" ([Plausible](https://plausible.io/#pricing)).
- Fathom: $15/month up to 100k pageviews, 7-day trial with card, "No cookies, so no consent banner for analytics" ([Fathom](https://usefathom.com/pricing)).
- Umami Cloud: Hobby free (100k events, 1 site, 6-month retention), Pro $20/month (1M events) ([Umami](https://umami.is/pricing)).
- Caveat: these are vendor claims; EU regulators have not uniformly blessed cookieless analytics (flagged).

## 5. Rewarded ads and incentives

- **Web rewarded ads exist in Google Ad Manager**: trafficked on "desktop, mobile, and tablet web inventory"; the publisher builds the opt-in screen and delivers the reward; ads must be marked rewarded and closed manually ([Ad Manager](https://support.google.com/admanager/answer/9116812)). Ad Manager sign-up requires "a valid Google AdSense account" ([Ad Manager](https://support.google.com/admanager/answer/7084151)) and is aimed at publishers with "significant direct sales" ([Ad Manager](https://support.google.com/admanager/answer/6022000)). AdSense publishes the same rewarded policies ([AdSense](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9121589)) but a web rewarded unit in ordinary AdSense display is not documented — unverified. Ezoic sells web rewarded/ad-gated content, subject to its eligibility ([Ezoic](https://www.ezoic.com/rewarded-ads)).
- **Reward policy**: user must "affirmatively and unambiguously" opt in; disclose action and reward before each instance; must be skippable; "Direct monetary items may not be offered as rewards under any circumstance"; non-monetary rewards must be non-transferable; physical-item discounts capped at 25% ([policy](https://support.google.com/admanager/answer/7496282)).
- **Prohibited incentives**: "offering rewards to users for clicking ads" and asking users to click are account-closure grounds ([AdSense](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/2660562)).
- **FTC Consumer Review Rule** (effective Oct 21, 2024): incentives conditioned on sentiment are banned; incentives not conditioned on sentiment are allowed, but "failing to disclose incentives could be a violation of the FTC Act"; paying for 5-star reviews even with disclosure violates §465.4 ([FTC Q&A](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers)); "Don't ask for reviews only from customers you think will leave positive ones" ([FTC marketers guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/soliciting-paying-online-reviews-guide-marketers)). Civil penalties up to $53,088 per violation (2025 adjustment) ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/02/ftc-publishes-inflation-adjusted-civil-penalty-amounts-2025)); the FTC warned 10 companies in Dec 2025 ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/12/ftc-warns-10-companies-about-possible-violations-agencys-new-consumer-review-rule)).
- **Recommendation for a tiny app**: skip rewarded ads for now (Ad Manager overhead, needs AdSense first, tiny inventory). If revisited, reward only non-monetary in-app perks (e.g., unlock a premium template or extra export) behind an explicit opt-in with the action/reward stated. Collect feedback with no incentive, or a small sentiment-neutral, disclosed one offered to everyone; never pay for ratings/reviews or tie anything to ad clicks.

## 6. ads.txt, sellers.json, placement and performance

- **ads.txt**: place at the domain root with `google.com, pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX, DIRECT, f08c47fec0942fa0`; "not mandatory, but it's highly recommended"; AdSense alerts on missing/unauthorized IDs ([AdSense](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/12171612)). Works on a static host.
- **sellers.json**: choose Transparent (name, domain, publisher ID published within 24 h) or Confidential; Google warns that staying confidential "might impact your revenue" ([AdSense](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9889911)). Transparent publishes a sole proprietor's legal name — a privacy trade-off.
- **Better Ads Standards**: desktop disallows pop-ups, auto-playing video with sound, prestitials with countdown, large sticky ads, ad density >50% (>30% with sticky video); mobile disallows pop-ups, prestitials, density >30%, flashing animated ads, auto-play video with sound, poststitials with countdown, full-screen scrollovers, large sticky ads, sticky pop-out video ([Coalition for Better Ads](https://www.betterads.org/standards/)). Mobile density = sum of ad heights ÷ main-content height ([AdPushup](https://www.adpushup.com/blog/ad-density/), third-party). A "Failing" Ad Experience Report leads Chrome to filter all ads on the site ([Google](https://support.google.com/webtools/answer/7642788)).
- **AdSense controls**: anchor, vignette (interstitial) and side-rail overlays can each be switched off; a slider caps in-page ad count ([AdSense Auto ads settings](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9305577)). Policies forbid more ads than publisher content on a screen and require honest labels such as "Advertisements" or "Sponsored Links" ([program policies](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/48182)).
- **Performance**: load the ad script `async`; reserve fixed-size slots (`min-height`/`aspect-ratio`); keep the reserved space with a placeholder when no ad fills ("Removing the space set aside for elements can cause just as much CLS as inserting content"); inject lower in the viewport; lazy-load below-the-fold slots with `loading=lazy` ([web.dev loading ads](https://web.dev/articles/loading-ads-page-speed), [web.dev CLS](https://web.dev/articles/optimize-cls)).

## Suggested sequence

1. Now: affiliate links (Amazon, Backcountry, REI via AvantLink, The Dyrt) with per-link "Paid link" disclosures and a sentence of commentary per item; Umami Hobby or Plausible for analytics; no display ads.
2. Add 5–10 text-rich guide pages (one per template) before applying to AdSense; keep ads off the app screen itself; ship ads.txt; enable RDP for US and Google's certified GDPR message for EU.
3. At ~1,000 US sessions/month apply to Journey; at 25k pageviews Raptive.
4. Storage tier: $24/yr (or $2.99/mo), Stripe Checkout + Link, Cloudflare Workers + R2.
