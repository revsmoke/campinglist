Smokies Backcountry Camping

For backpackers spending one to four nights at designated backcountry campsites and shelters in Great Smoky Mountains National Park.

  • SeasonMar-Nov (peak Apr-May and Oct); wet year-round

Assumes a spring-to-fall backpacking loop for up to 8 people using reserved backcountry campsites or shelters. Covers the Recreation.gov permit (new $6 fee and system as of April 30, 2026), mandatory bear-cable food storage, shelter rules and the park's famously wet weather. Appalachian Trail thru-hikers use a different permit; winter trips need more insulation and traction.

Check before you go

  • All backcountry camping requires an advance reservation on Recreation.gov (from April 30, 2026): $8 per person per night plus a $6 non-refundable permit fee with no cap; reservations open 30 days ahead. The A.T. thru-hiker permit is $44.50.
  • Camp only at designated sites and shelters; max party 8; no more than 3 consecutive nights at a site and no consecutive nights at shelters or site 113. If your itinerary includes a shelter you must sleep inside it, and hammocks may not be used inside.
  • All odorous items (food, trash, toothpaste, lip balm) must hang on the bear cable system at every site; stay 50 yards from bears; use bear spray only if a bear is within 20 yards.
  • Fires only in existing rings at designated sites using dead-and-down wood or certified heat-treated firewood; never burn trash.
  • Expect rain in any month, slick roots and rock, and fast-moving cold fronts; hypothermia is a risk even in summer at elevation.
  • No soap of any kind in water sources; wash and bathe away from water and camp. Bury human waste 6 in deep at least 100 ft from water, camps and trails.
  • Check Trail Cautions and Closures for storm damage and bear-activity closures before you go.

Permits & Planning 5/7

  • Backcountry permit and reservation for every night Recreation.gov from April 30, 2026; $8/person/night plus $6 fee; reservable 30 days ahead.
  • Permit printed or saved and carried on the trail
  • Parking tag for any vehicle left in the park $5 daily, $15 weekly or $40 annual (as of 2026).
  • Itinerary matching reserved sites, shared with a contact
  • Check Trail Cautions and Closures and the forecast
  • Shuttle or car shuttle for point-to-point routes
  • Group of 8 or fewer; no two affiliated groups at one site

Food Storage & Bear Safety 3/6

  • Stuff sack and clip ready for the bear cablesCables are provided at every site; hang your whole food bag, pack or canister.
  • All smellables hung: toothpaste, lip balm, sunscreen, trash
  • Bear canister (optional in the Smokies; ATC recommends one)
  • Cook and eat away from the sleeping area
  • Bear sprayUse only if a bear is within 20 yards.
  • Meals repackaged, plus one extra day

Shelter & Sleep 3/5

  • Tent with full rainfly and footprintNeeded for campsite nights; shelter nights require sleeping inside the shelter.
  • Sleeping bag rated for the forecast
  • Sleeping pad
  • Earplugs (shelters are shared)
  • Hammock with straps for campsite nights onlyHammocks may not be used inside shelters.

Clothing 5/7

  • Rain jacket and rain pants
  • Non-cotton layers plus fleece or puffy
  • Wool socks (3 pairs) and dry camp socks
  • Boots or trail shoes with grip for wet roots and rock
  • Warm hat and gloves
  • Camp shoes
  • Pack cover and pack liner

Water & Kitchen 3/6

  • Water filter or treatment
  • Capacity for 2 liters
  • Stove and fuel
  • Cook kit
  • Lighter plus backup fire starter
  • Wash dishes well away from streams; no soap in water

Navigation & Safety 3/8

  • Park trail map
  • Headlamp with spare batteries
  • First aid kit
  • Whistle
  • Trekking poles
  • Satellite messenger
  • Tick and insect repellent; nightly tick check
  • Sunscreen

Leave No Trace / Pack-out 3/5

  • Trowel for a 6 in cathole 100 ft from water, camp and trail
  • Pack out all trash and food scraps; never burn them
  • Pack out toilet paper or bury it deep in the cathole
  • Fires only in existing rings with dead-and-down or heat-treated wood
  • Leave shelters clean with no food stored inside
