Bonnaroo Camping

For festival-goers car camping or tent camping for four days on the Bonnaroo Farm in Manchester, Tennessee.

Assumes a group of two to four arriving Wednesday or Thursday for Primitive Car Camping (about a 20 ft by 20 ft plot including the vehicle) during the mid-June festival (June 11-14 in 2026). Covers tickets and camping passes, the campground allowed and prohibited items list, Tennessee heat and storms, and shade and water logistics. Adapt for RV, glamping or Groop camping and re-check the official allowed-items list each year.

Check before you go

  • Everyone needs a Centeroo ticket plus one camping and parking pass per vehicle; Primitive Car Camping plots are roughly 20 by 20 ft including the car. Vehicle re-entry is discouraged and carried a $40 fee in 2026.
  • Campground rules (2026 list): glass, wood fires, kegs, weapons, drones, fireworks, bikes and scooters, pets (except service animals), glow sticks and laser pointers are prohibited; allowed are grills with up to 40 lb of charcoal or one 20 lb propane tank, generators under 4 kW with up to 10 gallons of gas, and personal alcohol (2 cases of beer, 2 boxes of wine, 1 to 1.75 L of liquor per person per car). Verify the current list.
  • Centeroo (the venue) allows empty reusable bottles and hydration packs, blankets and point-and-shoot cameras but bans outside food and drink, chairs, wagons, bikes and backpacks with multiple pockets; check the current Centeroo list before packing a day bag.
  • June in Tennessee: 90 F heat with high humidity, strong sun and thunderstorms with lightning and mud; tents are hot by 8 am, so a canopy and a fan matter.
  • Free water refill stations are spread through Outeroo and Centeroo; showers at the plazas cost a small fee. Bring enough water for the drive in and the entry line.
  • Dates, camping tiers (Groop, Mars and Moon Colony tents, RV, Accessible) and policies change yearly; check bonnaroo.com and the Help Center before packing.

Tickets & Planning 6/8

  • Centeroo ticket or wristband for every person
  • Camping and parking pass for the vehicle (one per campsite) Covers everyone in the car; each person still needs a Centeroo ticket.
  • Photo ID and a credit card
  • Arrival day (Wed or Thu) and campground map reviewed
  • Group meeting spot and a flag or landmark for your site
  • Read the current allowed and prohibited lists for Outeroo and Centeroo
  • Bonnaroo app downloaded for schedules and alerts
  • Vehicle packed for a quick search: no glass, alcohol within limits

Shelter & Shade 5/8

  • Tent that fits a 20 by 20 ft plot beside the car
  • Pop-up canopy (10 by 10 ft) with weights or stakesTents become ovens by 8 am; the canopy is the escape.
  • Tarps or sidewalls for extra shade
  • Sleeping pad or air mattress with pump
  • Light sleeping bag or sheet
  • Battery-powered fan
  • Camp chairs
  • Rain fly, extra stakes and mallet for storms

Water & Hydration 2/5

  • Reusable water bottle or hydration pack (empty when entering Centeroo)
  • Gallon jugs of water for camp
  • Electrolyte packets
  • Cooler with ice (ice is sold on site)
  • Personal misting fan

Food & Kitchen 2/6

  • Propane grill or stove with a 20 lb or smaller tankOne tank per grill per vehicle, or two small Coleman-style canisters (2026 rules).
  • Charcoal, 40 lb or less per grillNo wood fires.
  • Food and snacks for the stay
  • Alcohol within per-person limits, no glass
  • Utensils, plates and trash bags
  • Hand sanitizer and wipes

Clothing & Sun 4/7

  • Sunscreen SPF 30+ and lip balm
  • Hat and sunglasses
  • Comfortable broken-in shoes plus rain boots or sandals
  • Rain poncho or jacket
  • Light breathable clothes and bandanas
  • Warm layer for night
  • Earplugs and eye mask

Health & Safety 4/7

  • First aid kit
  • Prescriptions in original containers
  • Factory-sealed naloxone (Narcan) kit
  • Phone, battery bank and solar charger
  • Headlamp or flashlight and a lantern
  • Group totem under 7 ft
  • Locks for tent and car; valuables stashed out of sight

Centeroo Day Bag 1/4

  • Small bag that meets current Centeroo bag rulesBackpacks with multiple pockets were banned inside Centeroo in 2026.
  • Empty bottle, sunscreen, poncho, phone, ID and cash
  • Blanket or towel
  • Earplugs

Leave No Trace / Pack-out 4/5

  • Trash and recycling bags
  • Leave the campsite clean using the provided dumpsters and recycling
  • Take tents, canopies and chairs home instead of abandoning them
  • Portable ashtray for cigarette butts
  • No glass and no wood fires at camp
