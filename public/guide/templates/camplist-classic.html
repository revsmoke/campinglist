A general-purpose list for a weekend to a week of car camping at a developed or semi-developed campground. It covers planning tasks, shelter, kitchen, clothing, first aid, hygiene, navigation, tools and campfire gear. Trim sections you do not need and add trip-specific items from the other templates.

Check before you go

  • Check the campground's current fire restrictions and firewood rules before you buy or bring wood.
  • Confirm reservation, quiet-hours and check-in rules for your specific campground; they vary by season.
  • Review the weather forecast 48 hours before departure and adjust clothing and shelter accordingly.
  • Food-storage rules (bears, raccoons) differ by region; follow posted guidance at your site.

Pre-Trip Planning Script 12/13

  • Pick destination & reserve campsite
  • Check permit rules (fires, fishing, drones)
  • Verify seasonal hazards (bears, fire bans, bugs)
  • Inspect/repair tent, stakes, zippers
  • Test camp-stove & fuel canisters
  • Create shared food plan & divide groceries
  • Check 7-day weather forecast & adjust clothing
  • Download offline maps & GPX route
  • Charge power banks, camera batteries, headlamps
  • Freeze one gallon of drinking water (acts as ice)
  • Pre-pack cooler & label meal bags
  • Put house on away-mode (thermostat, trash, mail)
  • Turn off stove, lock doors, set alarm
